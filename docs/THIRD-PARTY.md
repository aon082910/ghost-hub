## JustDeleteMe account-deletion guides

`src/lib/deletion/guides.json` is derived from the [JustDeleteMe](https://github.com/jdm-contrib/jdm) dataset
(`_data/sites.json`), trimmed to the fields Ghost-Hub shows. It is licensed under the MIT License, reproduced in
[`src/lib/deletion/JUSTDELETEME-LICENSE.txt`](../src/lib/deletion/JUSTDELETEME-LICENSE.txt):

Refresh it with `npm run guides:update`, which downloads the current dataset and its license text. The information
is maintained by volunteers and may be out of date.
